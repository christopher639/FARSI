# Welcome to MY NIRU AI project: Forensic Analysis Reat-Time security Intelligence Platform (FARSI)

## Project info

-- Project Details

-- Problem Statement

Kenya's security apparatus faces a multifaceted threat environment, from terrorism and cross-border crime to urban violence and livestock rustling. A critical operational gap persists despite the vast data collected by agencies like the National Police Service (NPS), National Intelligence Service (NIS), and others. This gap is characterized by three key challenges: Information Silos: Vital intelligence is often trapped within individual agencies, preventing a unified, holistic view of the security landscape. A police report in Garissa, a financial transaction in Nairobi, and a border alert in Mandera may all be connected, but the silos prevent this linkage. Data Overload and Manual Analysis: Security forces are inundated with unstructured data—text reports, intercepted communications, CCTV footage, and satellite imagery. Manual analysis of this data is slow, inefficient, and prone to human error, leading to delayed responses and missed patterns. Reactive, Not Proactive, Posture: The current paradigm is largely reactive. Security operations are often mobilized after an incident has occurred. There is a lack of advanced analytical tools to fuse disparate data streams and generate predictive, actionable intelligence that can prevent attacks. This siloed, slow, and reactive approach hampers Kenya's ability to pre-empt threats, optimize resource deployment, and ensure the safety of its citizens and stability of the nation.

Proposed Solution
We propose FARSI, an AI-powered platform designed to transform Kenya's security operations from reactive to predictive. FARSI is a unified intelligence framework that breaks down data silos, automates analysis, and delivers actionable insights in real-time. The solution is built on three integrated pillars: Pillar 1: Secure, Multi-Modal Data Fusion Hub FARSI provides a centralized platform with secure API gateways for authorized agencies to contribute data without relinquishing control. It is designed to handle Kenya's diverse data landscape: Structured Data: Crime statistics, arrest records, vehicle registries. Unstructured Text: Police reports (in Swahili and English), court documents, and open-source intelligence (news, social media). Multi-Media: CCTV footage, aerial imagery, and intercepted audio communications. Pillar 2: The AI Intelligence Engine This is the core of FARSI, featuring specialized, explainable AI modules: Natural Language Processing (NLP) Module: This module is trained on Swahili and English text. It automatically analyzes thousands of reports to perform Named Entity Recognition (NER), identifying and linking people, organizations, locations, and vehicles. It also conducts Sentiment Analysis on social media to detect early warnings of civil unrest or radicalization narratives. Computer Vision Module: This module processes video and image data. Its capabilities include: Facial Recognition: Matching suspect images against secured, authorized watchlists. Object &amp; Vehicle Recognition: Identifying suspicious vehicles (e.g., a specific model used in a prior attack) or unattended baggage in real-time from CCTV feeds. Anomaly Detection: Flagging unusual crowd behavior (e.g., sudden dispersal, gathering) in public spaces. Predictive Analytics &amp; Network Mapping Module: Using graph neural networks, this module is the predictive brain of FARSI. It connects entities extracted by the NLP and Vision modules to map complex criminal networks. It identifies key influencers, hidden connections, and vulnerabilities within these networks. Furthermore, by fusing historical incident data with real-time feeds, it generates predictive threat heatmaps, visually identifying high-probability areas for future incidents like IED placements or armed raids. Pillar 3: Secure Collaboration &amp; Command Dashboard FARSI delivers its insights through a role-based, unified dashboard for command centers and field operatives. Real-Time Alerts: Automated alerts are pushed for flagged persons, vehicles, or predicted threats. Interactive Threat Heatmaps: Visual, map-based displays of predictive analytics for strategic resource allocation. Secure Messaging: Encrypted, in-platform communication for coordinated action. Audit Trail: Every user action is logged to ensure accountability and prevent intelligence leaks. Data Protection &amp; Ethics: FARSI is designed with a "Privacy by Design" approach. It incorporates data anonymization techniques (blurring faces, redacting PII) and can utilize Federated Learning, allowing AI models to be trained on agency servers without raw data ever leaving their premises, thus preserving the highest levels of data sovereignty and security.

Relevance to Theme
FARSI is directly and powerfully relevant to the theme of Security, Safety and Policing as it redefines the core operational capabilities of security agencies. Proactive Policing &amp; Crime Prevention: By moving from a reactive to a predictive model, FARSI enables security forces to prevent incidents. Deploying patrols to a predicted hotspot disrupts criminal plans before they are executed, saving lives and property. Enhanced Investigative Capabilities: The AI-powered network mapping uncovers hidden connections between suspects that would take human analysts weeks to find. This dramatically speeds up investigations into complex crimes like terrorism, trafficking, and organized crime rings. Optimized Resource Deployment: Security resources are finite. FARSI's predictive heatmaps and intelligence-led prioritization ensure that personnel and assets are deployed where they are most needed and will be most effective, maximizing operational impact and fiscal responsibility. Inter-Agency Synergy: FARSI directly addresses the crippling problem of information silos. By creating a secure, trusted platform for data sharing and collaboration, it fosters unprecedented synergy between the NPS, NIS, KWS, and other agencies, creating a unified front against national security threats. Safety for Officers and Citizens: By providing superior situational awareness and early warnings, FARSI helps keep both security personnel and the public safe. Officers are less likely to walk into ambushes, and citizens are protected by a more responsive and intelligent security infrastructure.

Thematic Area
Security, Safety, and Policing

Technology & Methodology
AI/ML Frameworks: TensorFlow, PyTorch for model development. SpaCy and Hugging Face for NLP. OpenCV for computer vision. Neo4j for graph databases. Backend &amp; Data Engineering: Python (Django/FastAPI), PostgreSQL, Elasticsearch, Apache Kafka for real-time data streaming. Infrastructure &amp; Deployment: Docker and Kubernetes for containerization and scalability. Designed for deployment on a Government-Private Cloud to ensure data sovereignty. Security: End-to-end encryption (TLS), OAuth 2.0/OpenID Connect for authentication, and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Methodology: Agile development, focusing on an initial Minimum Viable Product (MVP) for the hackathon that demonstrates predictive threat heatmapping.
